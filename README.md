I'm a Pakistani CS researcher based in Ürümqi, China, doing my master's at Xinjiang University. My research is in **visual anomaly detection** — teaching models to spot defects and anomalies in images across industrial, logical, and medical domains.

Right now I'm working on combining **continual learning** and **parameter-efficient fine-tuning** (LoRA, adapters, visual prompts) on vision foundation models like DINOv2 and CLIP. The goal: models that can keep adapting to new domains without forgetting what they already know.

Before the master's I fine-tuned BERT for production NLP at **Bytewise** and built an end-to-end AI platform as my final year project — got a 4.0/4.0 for it, which I'm still pretty happy about.

---

### 🔬 What I'm working on

- **RetriVAD** — training-free unified visual anomaly detection via single-encoder retrieval
- Reproducing & extending state-of-the-art VAD methods on MVTec-AD, MVTec-LOCO, and VisA
- Building PEFT pipelines on DINOv2 and CLIP for domain-adaptive anomaly detection
